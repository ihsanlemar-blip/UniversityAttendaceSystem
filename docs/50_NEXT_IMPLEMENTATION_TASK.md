## 1. Task Objective

Implement **Milestone 15: Physical QR Student Cards, Hardware Scanning & Attendance Reporting** for the Digital Student Attendance System. Milestone 15 introduces inclusive attendance mechanisms for students without supported smartphones or experiencing temporary device failure via cryptographically signed physical student ID cards (ADR-018), authorized staff-scanned checkpoint verification, and institutional attendance analytics/reporting.

---

## 2. Invariants & Guardrails (Mandatory)

1. **Staff-Scanned Only for Physical Cards (INV-01 & ADR-018)**:
   - Physical student QR cards can only be scanned and submitted by authorized lecturers, attendance officers, or administrators present in the active session room.
   - Students cannot scan their own or other students' physical cards (rejected with HTTP 403 `UNAUTHORIZED_SCANNER`).
2. **Cryptographic Card Signing & Tamper Proofing**:
   - Physical cards encode university ID, student ID, card serial number, issue timestamp, and an HMAC-SHA256 signature generated by a dedicated university card signing key.
   - Counterfeit or forged cards presented during attendance checkpoints are rejected immediately and trigger an anti-cheat risk signal.
3. **Audit History & Replacement Revocation**:
   - Replaced or reported lost physical cards are marked `REVOKED` in the database; scans of revoked cards are rejected.
   - Every physical card scan is flagged with evidence mode `STAFF_SCANNED_PHYSICAL_CARD` and logs the scanning staff member's actor ID in the immutable audit ledger.
4. **Preserve Fast Testing Strategy**:
   - Sharded CI across 4 parallel jobs with real PostgreSQL 16 containers.
   - Zero real-world sleeps (`time.sleep()` or `asyncio.sleep()`).
   - Pure UTC timestamps and mocked authoritative clocks.

2. **Card Issuance & Cryptographic Encoding**:

3. **Lecturer & Officer Hardware Scanning Flow**:
   - Mobile and Web "Scan Student Card" interface with camera and external barcode reader support.
   - Backend endpoint `POST /api/v1/attendance/card/scan` requiring staff session authority.
4. **Institutional Reporting & Export**:
   - Course-level, faculty-level, and student-level attendance aggregation.
   - CSV / Excel / PDF official attendance record exports.
